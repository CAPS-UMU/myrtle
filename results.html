The following tile selection algorithm works for Square Matmuls (M=N=K):

  1. Prune all tiling schemes with SSR Configs <= 2nd Smallest SSR Configs
  2. Select tile by minimizing ratio of regular to streaming loads per core.

    3. Note that in example M=N=K=384, there exists a point with a smaller ratio of reg:stream. We discard this point by pruning to 2nd smallest SSR Configs beforehand.
